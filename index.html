How booking works

  1. Choose your bikePick a bike, then your pickup and return time. The price updates as you choose.
  2. Fill in the booking formYour details, Aadhaar and driving licence number. No online payment is taken.
  3. We verify youWe check your details and email you the result. You can follow progress with your booking code.
  4. RideWe deliver the bike to your address, or you collect it from our showroom.

Keep these ready

  • Aadhaar card (original at handover)
  • A valid two-wheeler driving licence
  • Age 18 or above
  • A working phone number and email
